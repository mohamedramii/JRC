Established in 2017, Jordan River Center for Certified Translation (JRC) is a fully licensed translation company based in Amman, Jordan, operating in accordance with the laws of the Hashemite Kingdom of Jordan. We provide certified translation, interpretation, and comprehensive language services to businesses, government entities, international organizations, and individuals.

Guided by the highest standards of professionalism, accuracy, confidentiality, reliability, and punctuality, we deliver exceptional language services tailored to our clients' unique requirements.

Our services include certified written translation, legal and technical translation, document translation, and simultaneous and consecutive interpreting in Arabic, English, and other languages upon request.

A key strength of JRC is our specialization in military, defense, and security interpretation. We provide highly qualified military interpreters for simultaneous and consecutive interpreting, along with integrated language support solutions for military exercises, field operations, and international cooperation programs.

At Jordan River Center for Certified Translation (JRC), we believe that language is a bridge for communication and cooperation across cultures, and that professional translation plays a vital role in building understanding and strengthening partnerships. With our specialized expertise and comprehensive language services, we help our clients overcome language barriers and establish clear, effective, and reliable communication.

With a commitment to delivering high-quality services, we maintain the highest standards of accuracy, confidentiality, professionalism, reliability, and punctuality, delivering tailored language solutions that meet our clients’ needs and exceed their expectations.
