At Jordan River Center for Certified Translation (JRC), we believe that professional translation goes beyond simply transferring words from one language to another. It is a responsibility that demands accuracy, expertise, and a commitment to the highest professional standards.

Since our establishment in 2017, we have been dedicated to providing comprehensive translation solutions that meet the needs of individuals, businesses, government institutions, and international organizations. We build on our extensive experience, specialized team, and broad network of qualified translators and interpreters to deliver reliable, accurate, and professional language services.

Guided by the highest standards of professionalism, accuracy, confidentiality, reliability, and punctuality, we deliver exceptional language services tailored to our clients' unique requirements.

Our services include certified written translation, legal and technical translation, document translation, and simultaneous and consecutive interpreting in Arabic, English, and other languages upon request.

A key strength of JRC is our specialization in military, defense, and security interpretation. We provide highly qualified military interpreters for simultaneous and consecutive interpreting, along with integrated language support solutions for military exercises, field operations, and international cooperation programs.

At Jordan River Center for Certified Translation (JRC), we believe that language is a bridge for communication and cooperation across cultures, and that professional translation plays a vital role in building understanding and strengthening partnerships. With our specialized expertise and comprehensive language services, we help our clients overcome language barriers and establish clear, effective, and reliable communication.

With a commitment to delivering high-quality services, we maintain the highest standards of accuracy, confidentiality, professionalism, reliability, and punctuality, delivering tailored language solutions that meet our clients’ needs and exceed their expectations.

تُعد الترجمة الفورية العسكرية والدفاعية والأمنية إحدى أبرز نقاط التميز التي ينفرد بها مركز نهر الأردن للترجمة المعتمدة، حيث نوفر مترجمين فوريين عسكريين ذوي خبرة وكفاءة عالية لتقديم خدمات الترجمة الفورية والتتابعية، إلى جانب توفير حلول دعم لغوي متكاملة للتمارين العسكرية، والعمليات الميدانية، وبرامج التعاون الدولي.

في مركز نهر الأردن للترجمة المعتمدة (JRC)، نؤمن بأن اللغة هي جسر للتواصل والتعاون بين الثقافات، وأن الترجمة الاحترافية تُسهم إسهامًا حيويًا في بناء التفاهم وتعزيز الشراكات. بفضل خبرتنا المتخصصة وخدماتنا اللغوية الشاملة، نساعد عملاءنا على التغلب على الحواجز اللغوية وإقامة اتصالات واضحة وفعالة وموثوقة.

وانطلاقًا من التزامنا بتقديم خدمات عالية الجودة، فإننا نحافظ على أعلى معايير الدقة والسرية والاحترافية والموثوقية والالتزام بالمواعيد، ونقدم حلولاً لغوية مخصصة تلبي احتياجات عملائنا وتتجاوز توقعاتهم.
