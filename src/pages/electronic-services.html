Navigating government procedures and completing official transactions can often be time-consuming and require familiarity with different platforms and administrative requirements.

At Jordan River Center For Certified Translation, we simplify these processes by providing electronic government services and administrative support, helping individuals complete their transactions with greater convenience, efficiency and confidence.

Our services cover a range of government procedures, from completing electronic applications and forms to following up on transactions, booking appointments and assisting with official documentation.

We also provide support with the Sanad government platform and application, helping clients navigate its services and access the electronic government services they need. Our assistance extends to transactions with various ministries and government institutions, including Greater Amman Municipality and the Department of Lands and Survey.

Whether you need assistance submitting an application, following up on an existing transaction or certifying official documents, our team is committed to making the process easier and more straightforward.

Our Specialized Services

  • Government Applications & Forms: Completing and submitting electronic government applications and forms, processing transactions and facilitating fee payments.
  • Government Transaction Follow-Up: Enquiries about government transactions, tracking their progress and booking appointments with ministries and government institutions.
  • Sanad Platform Support: Assistance with using the Sanad government platform and application, including completing and following up on transactions with various government departments.
  • Official Document Certification: Certification of official documents and records, along with follow-up on related procedures with the relevant government authorities.
