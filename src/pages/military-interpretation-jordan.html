Military, Defense & Law Enforcement Linguistic Solutions

Jordan River Center for Certified Translation (JRC) specializes in providing military, defense, and law enforcement interpretation and linguistic support services. Through its network of qualified military and police training interpreters, multilingual linguists, and subject-matter experts, JRC supports mission-critical communication across the defense, security, and law enforcement sectors.

JRC delivers professional simultaneous and consecutive interpretation, field interpretation, and specialized military and law enforcement terminology support to government agencies, defense contractors, embassies, NGOs, and private-sector clients. Our services include military and police interpreter staffing for training programs, operational activities, joint military exercises, conferences, official engagements, and other mission-related events throughout Jordan, the Middle East, and international deployment locations.

PAST PERFORMANCE:

Jordan River Center for Certified Translation (JRC) has provided qualified military and police training interpreters across multiple languages in support of the U.S. Department of State’s Antiterrorism Assistance (ATA) Program in Jordan, supporting specialized security, law enforcement, and counterterrorism training programs.

Services have included interpreter recruitment, mission-specific preparation, deployment coordination, quality assurance, performance monitoring, and ongoing linguistic support, meeting the operational, security, and training requirements of U.S. Government contractors and international partners.

Potential Program Support & Deployment Capabilities

JRC is positioned to provide qualified military, police, and law enforcement interpreters in support of U.S. Department of State-funded programs, including ATA, GATA, SPEAR, law enforcement capacity-building, and counterterrorism training activities in Jordan and across the wider Middle East region
