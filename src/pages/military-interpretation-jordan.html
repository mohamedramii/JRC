Military and operational settings require clear communication and an understanding of specialized terminology. Jordan River Center for Certified Translation (JRC) provides certified military interpreters for consecutive and simultaneous interpretation, supporting communication during training, exercises, meetings, and other activities where military knowledge and context are important.

The nature of military assignments can vary significantly. Interpretation may be needed for structured training sessions, exercises, conferences, workshops, or operational support activities. We take the assignment context and requirements into consideration when discussing interpreter support and deployment.

This service includes:

  • Certified military interpreters with specialized military experience and knowledge in relevant fields.
  • The ability to provide and deploy two qualified military interpreters promptly according to mission and project requirements.
  • Interpretation support for training programs, exercises, military maneuvers, conferences, meetings, workshops, seminars, and operational support activities.

When is military interpretation required?

This service may support military training programs, joint exercises, maneuvers, meetings, conferences, and related activities involving participants who communicate in different languages.

Our approach

We discuss the assignment’s scope, language needs, subject matter, location, schedule, and deployment requirements to understand the support needed. Interpreter availability and arrangements are coordinated according to the specific mission or project.
