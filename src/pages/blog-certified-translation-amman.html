Certified translation is an essential service for individuals and businesses dealing with official authorities in Jordan or abroad. Whether you are preparing to travel, applying to study overseas, completing immigration procedures, or handling a legal matter, accurate certified translation helps you submit clear documents that meet the receiving organization’s requirements.

At Jordan River Center for Certified Translation, we understand that official translation involves more than transferring words from one language to another. What Is Certified Translation?

Certified translation is the translation of an official document into another language while meeting the certification requirements of the receiving organization. Depending on the document and destination, a transaction may require a signature, stamp, or additional attestations.

Requirements vary between organizations. It is therefore advisable to check the instructions of the relevant embassy, university, or institution before arranging a translation.

Which Documents May Require Certified Translation?

Document requirements depend on the purpose of the transaction. Common examples include:

  • Birth, marriage, and family documents.
  • University degrees and academic transcripts.
  • Passports and personal identification documents.
  • Legal documents, contracts, and powers of attorney.
  • Visa and immigration documents.
  • Medical reports.
  • Business documents and company records.

Each document is translated with attention to its specific terminology and format, while names, dates, numbers, and other details are carefully rendered.

Why Accuracy Matters in Certified Translation

An error in a name, date, or number on an official document may delay a process or require a corrected translation. Certified translation therefore calls for close attention to detail, not just language proficiency.

  • Reviewing the source document and understanding its purpose.
  • Using terminology appropriate to the document.
  • Preserving names, numbers, and dates as they appear in the original.
  • Reviewing the translation for clarity and consistency.
  • Considering the receiving organization’s requirements when available.

How to Choose a Certified Translation Office in Amman

When looking for certified translation services in Amman, Jordan, choose a provider that prioritizes accuracy and clarity and explains the requirements before work begins.

Ask whether the translation will meet the receiving organization’s requirements, which language is needed, and whether additional attestation is required. Providing a clear copy of the document can also help establish the scope, cost, and expected turnaround time.
