Clear communication makes meetings, conferences, and events more productive—especially when participants speak different languages. Jordan River Center for Certified Translation (JRC) provides consecutive and simultaneous interpretation to help people communicate across language barriers while keeping discussions understandable and engaging.

Interpretation needs vary according to the setting. Some events require consecutive interpretation, with the speaker pausing to allow the message to be conveyed; others call for simultaneous interpretation, delivered as the speaker continues. We consider the event format, subject matter, language combination, and audience needs when arranging interpretation support.

This service includes:

  • Certified consecutive and simultaneous interpreters.
  • Interpreters with professional experience and specialized knowledge across different fields.
  • Interpretation for conferences, meetings, training programs, workshops, seminars, commercial events, and official activities.
  • Support for government entities, companies, legal, technical, medical, and educational sectors, international development organizations, NGOs, and military training programs.

Where can interpretation be used?

Our interpretation services can support multilingual conferences, business meetings, training sessions, workshops, seminars, and official or commercial events where accurate communication is important.

Our approach

We work to understand the event’s purpose, format, languages, and subject matter so that interpretation support aligns with the communication needs of participants. Share event details and requirements when you contact us to discuss the appropriate arrangement.
