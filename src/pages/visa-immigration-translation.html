Visa and immigration applications often involve documents that must be presented clearly and in the required language. Jordan River Center for Certified Translation (JRC) offers translation and document-preparation support to help applicants organize the paperwork associated with their applications and prepare materials for the relevant process.

Requirements can differ based on destination, application type, and the applicant’s circumstances. For this reason, it is helpful to identify the destination and the documents involved before work begins. Our service focuses on translation and preparation support for the document types outlined below.

This service includes:

  • Translation and preparation of visa and immigration documents.
  • Translation and preparation of documents for Schengen visa applications.
  • Preparation of documents for U.S. visa applications.

Who may need this service?

Individuals preparing visa or immigration applications who need assistance translating or organizing supporting documents may find this service useful. Applicants should confirm the current requirements directly with the relevant embassy, consulate, or authority.

Our approach

We begin by understanding the destination, application category, and documents you need to prepare. Please share any checklist or instructions issued by the relevant authority so that the requested support can be discussed accurately. Translation and document preparation do not guarantee visa approval.
